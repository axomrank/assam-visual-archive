# Historical film reference coverage audit

Audit snapshot on 5 November 2026. The census covers all 1,352 published
catalogue records across 80 collections and the registered Rang Ghar pack.
It checks what the archive actually records. Period and group labels are
discovery hints. Historical applicability requires separate source review.

sheets and reference collections. A historical claim about a community needs
its own period, locality, source and review.

Rang Ghar is a research pilot with two source-linked claims, two visual
assets and three proposed shots. The construction chronology is disputed.
The generated image changes the roof crest and openings. Wardrobe, language,
event sound and measured layout remain open. The audit found no completed,
reviewed production pack.

## Research queue

1. Close Rang Ghar's building chronology and measured-layout gaps before
   commissioning historical crowd, costume or action images.
2. Build a 15th-century everyday-life pack and a dated 20th-century pack
   to test how the archive handles very different kinds of evidence.
3. Work through the undated portraits, objects and photo records in
   screenplay priority order. Record evidence at claim level, with capture
   date separate from depiction date.
4. Add historical community records with local review and commission
   illustrations only after the evidence and gaps are documented.
5. Re-run the audit at each pack release. Track structured source, locality,
   period and permission coverage alongside image counts.

## Scope limits

This is a full metadata census of the published catalogue and registered
packs. It is not an independent authenticity review of every source or
a census of the private working archive. Source-link counts measure presence,
not live availability. The optional local photo manifest enriches monument
and view tags. Running from a public clone without that manifest leaves
those tags empty.
